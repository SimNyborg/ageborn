## What we learned

1. **The dilemma works.** DV18 has the lowest skip rate (44 %) and the longest average watch (18 s) of all 47
   posts, with n=1. It is the default devlog format until three dilemmas say otherwise.
2. **Steve v4 is not hurting.** The three v4 posts (DV16-DV18) have median skip 54 % against 62 % for DV8-DV15. Too few to call it a win.
3. **The real limit is production, not ideas.** Every lost day came from the pipeline: restarts and per-call
   approvals. The account grows only when posts go out.
