## What we learned

1. **A visible problem or a twist wins reach** (DV3, DV5, DV1: 185-299). An explanation (DV4) does not.
2. **Before/after holds viewers** (40-42 % watch at 31-33 s) but has not yet broken out on reach.
3. **Duels are a safe floor** (about 140-170 reach, high watch) and the cheapest to make.
4. **Repeats are punished** (R10). Space similar formats at least 3 days apart.
5. **The ceiling moved**: from about 120 in week 1 to about 150-190, with one post at 299.
