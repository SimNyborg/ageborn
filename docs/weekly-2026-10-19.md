## What we learned

1. **Short beats long on watch share, not on reach** (format test, n=3 vs 6): short-hook duels hold 40 %
   vs 27 %, but reach is the same. Short-hook is now the default for battle content; classic battle Reels
   leave the mix.
2. **There is a reach ceiling (about 120).** Instagram shows each post to a first test batch and stops.
   Nothing we posted earned rewatches, shares or saves, which is what pushes a Reel further. This is the
   main problem, not the format.
3. **Watch share alone misleads.** It rewards short videos for being short. The bandit now scores the mean
   of watch share and seconds watched (capped at 10 s); with that, capsules, devlogs and duels are close.
4. **Devlogs hold attention longest** (6.6 s of 33 s). One post only; two more needed.
