Devlog #18: hold the line or charge? 🦌🪨
A dilemma I keep going back and forth on. Six cavemen against my Elk Chieftain. Option A: they charge and win, but only two are left. Option B: they hold the line and let the elk come to them. They win again, and four are still standing. Should holding the line be this strong, or will every battle turn into both sides waiting? A or B? 👇
